Bulmaca Gazetesi

Daily puzzles, newspaper style

Bulmaca Gazetesi brings classic newspaper puzzles to mobile. A fresh set of Sudoku, Kakuro and Number Fill In puzzles every day, plus Time Attack, Weekly Challenge and a weekly leaderboard.

Get it on Google Play

Features

  • A new daily puzzle set — the same puzzle for every player, every day
  • Three puzzle types across five difficulty bands
  • Classic mode and Newspaper mode
  • Weekly score leaderboard
  • Themes, paper textures and collectible cosmetics
  • Available in 10 languages
